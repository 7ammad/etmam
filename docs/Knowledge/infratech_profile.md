# Infratech: The Sovereign Shield
**Practitioner Playbook & Capability Matrix**

## 1. Strategic Fact Sheet
* **Core Identity:** A National Cybersecurity Authority (NCA) licensed service provider specializing in defensive and offensive cyber operations.
* **Primary Value Proposition:** "Sovereign Compliance." Infratech allows government entities to meet strict NCA and SAMA mandates through a locally hosted, NCA-licensed Managed Security Operations Center (mSOC).
* **Key Certifications & Licenses:**
    * **NCA License:** Authorized provider of Managed Security Services (MSSP).
    * **ISO Certifications:** ISO 27001 (InfoSec), ISO 9001 (Quality), ISO 20000 (IT Service Mgmt).
    * **Methodologies:** CREST/SANS aligned for penetration testing and incident response.
* **Strategic Clients (Verified):**
    * **Government:** Ministries requiring NCA compliance.
    * **Critical Infrastructure:** Utilities (Water/Electricity), Oil & Gas sectors.
