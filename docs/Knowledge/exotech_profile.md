# Exotech: The Intelligent Engine
**Practitioner Playbook & Capability Matrix**

## 1. Strategic Fact Sheet
* **Core Identity:** An emerging-technology partner focused on AI, Machine Learning, and Data Management, with strong alignment to National Data Management Office (NDMO) standards.
* **Primary Value Proposition:** "Sovereign Intelligence." Exotech builds AI and data platforms that are compliant with Saudi data residency and classification laws (NDMO/NAII), enabling government entities to adopt AI without risking data sovereignty.
* **Key Products:**
    * **SOC AI:** An autonomous security platform driven by generative AI, capable of running **air-gapped** (offline) for classified environments.
* **Strategic Clients (Verified):**
    * **NEOM:** Smart city and digital infrastructure projects.
    * **ZATCA:** Zakat, Tax and Customs Authority.
    * **MOMRAH:** Ministry of Municipal, Rural Affairs and Housing.
    * **Saudi Railway:** Transport logistics and data systems.
